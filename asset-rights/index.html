Mars, Jupiter and Neptune: Solar System Scope

We retained the source JPEGs and rendered spherical lighting, an artistic atmospheric rim, an 8192×4096 fallback, lighting images and thumbnails. The derived dimensions do not add source detail. Moon: NASA imagery

Moon from Space uses the 8192×4096 color map from the NASA CGI Moon Kit. Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio; Ernie Wright (USRA); LRO LROC/LOLA teams. The source combines LROC imagery with polar LOLA fill; its source adjustments and lower-resolution polar areas remain.

EnterLocus converted the 16-bit sRGB TIFF to an 8-bit RGB PNG while retaining its color profile, then rendered spherical lighting, an 8192×4096 fallback, a lighting image and a thumbnail. The slow rotation is an artistic loop, not a lunar ephemeris. These changes are recorded in the package provenance.

The NASA source imagery is identified separately from the CC BY 4.0 planet textures above. Consult the NASA Images and Media Usage Guidelines, including their third-party and endorsement provisions.
