Basics · 5 minutes

Set up your first Place.

A Place is one View for the scenery and one Room for the space around you. You can change either part whenever you like.

On this pageChoose a View and RoomChoose an experienceChange it later

Choose a View and Room

  1. Open Places. If you are already inside a Place, use the Places button in the Locus bar.
  2. Choose a Room card. Pick Your Room to keep your physical room around you, or choose a 3D Room to enter a complete virtual space.
  3. Choose a View card. This is the 360° scenery that appears beyond the Room or through your opened walls.
  4. Check the summary, then choose Enter. Locus opens the experience that matches the Room you selected.

Choose the experience that fits your room

Virtual SpaceChoose a 3D Room. Locus places you inside it and keeps the real desk area available where supported.
Room PortalChoose Your Room. Locus keeps your physical room present and lets you open detected walls onto the selected View.
Give yourself room to settle in. Clear the area around you and follow Apple Vision Pro safety guidance. A Room can offer several seats; choose the one whose table and sightline fit what you want to do.

Change it later

Open Places again and choose another Room or View. While you are already inside a Place, selecting a card applies the new choice immediately.

When you find a combination you like, continue with Tune and organize a Place to adjust the View, Room lighting, favorites, and tags.
