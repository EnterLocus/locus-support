Four places to settle into.

Locus 1.4.4 for Apple Vision Pro

The Mansions collection

Explore Mansions, a collection of four Supporter Rooms: Hilltop Villa, Aegean Terrace Estate, Maple Courtyard House, and Pacific Palapa House. Settle into a maple courtyard, a sunlit terrace, or a tropical open-air retreat.

Seats are clearer in larger Rooms

Choosing a seat is clearer in larger Rooms, with less clutter from distant seat groups and direct selection of partly hidden seats.

Water meets the Room

Water meets Room edges more cleanly and extends to the horizon from elevated seats. This update also fixes a thin bright line in panoramic skies and keeps Places out of the way when you open another control panel.

For Room authors

Complete Package v2 Rooms can now control how a selected View meets the Room: set the View ground height, decide whether View ground stays out of the Room footprint, and choose whether its midground is all visible, terrain only, or hidden. These controls need Locus 1.4.4 or later. Read the Room/View composition reference.
