Locus 1.4.3

Settle into Hilltop Villa.

A new Supporter Room with a double-height living area, a library, a glass Sky Room and a terrace with an infinity pool.

Available now for Apple Vision Pro

A Room to settle into

Hilltop Villa is a new Supporter Room with a double-height living area, a library, a glass Sky Room and a terrace with an infinity pool. Find it in Places with Locus 1.4.3.

A fuller fire

Room fireplaces now have fuller, more natural flames.

Smoother ways to move

Choosing seats in large Rooms is smoother, and water stays visible while you choose a spot to move to.

For Room authors

In Locus 1.4.3, a fireplace fire uses its authored footprint as a 3D volume. Place its anchor at the burner or fuel surface, or at the top of the logs; flames begin at the anchor’s local origin and rise along its local +Y. Read the Room particle effects reference.

Locus 1.4.3 is available on the App Store.
