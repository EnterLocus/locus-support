Locus 1.6.0

Open the doors, and let the light follow the day.

Pinch doors open in supported Rooms, let Room Lights switch automatically at sunset and sunrise, and step into rebuilt built-in Rooms with crisper lighting.

Locus 1.6.0 for Apple Vision Pro

Open doors in your Room

Pinch doors to open your Room to the view in supported Rooms like Crossbeam House, Cascade House, and Hilltop Villa. You can also switch doors and other room features from Current Place → Room Features.

Lights that follow the day

In day-and-night Views, Room Lights can now turn on automatically as the sun sets and off after sunrise. You can switch this anytime in Settings.

Refreshed built-in Rooms & crisper lighting

Atrium Loft, Courtyard Gallery, and Horizon Atelier have been rebuilt with updated layouts, new furnishings, and warmer per-lamp lighting. Across the Library, updated Rooms keep surface textures sharp even with the lights on—with smoother performance while moving around.

More ways to sort Places

Places adds New First and On Device First sorting toggles alongside Favorites First, and built-in Place details can open straight in Places.

For authors

Complete Package v2 Rooms can declare operable doors, pergola louvers, and fireplaces with interactiveStates (protocol floor 1.5.1, Locus 1.6.0 or later) and bake albedo-free lightmaps with lighting.bakedIndirect.mode: "irradiance" (protocol floor 1.5.2, Locus 1.6.0 or later) so full-resolution surface textures stay sharp under Room Lights. Views add large-scale skyRadiusMeters up to 1600 m plus View ambientAnimations and interactiveStates. Read the Complete Package v2 guide and baked irradiance specification, or use the updated public Room and View skills.
