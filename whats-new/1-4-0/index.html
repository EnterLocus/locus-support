Locus 1.4

Head into space.

Visit the Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn and Neptune from orbit, sit beside a black hole, and watch the sun glare brighten when you look at it. Places now groups related Places into series and marks new arrivals as New.

Available now for Apple Vision Pro

About this update

Locus 1.4 follows Locus 1.3.1 and is available on the App Store. It requires visionOS 27; on visionOS 26 you keep Locus 1.3.1. New Library content may arrive separately. Check your installed version in Settings → Help & About → About Locus.

A tour of the solar system.

The Library now has Views that put you in orbit around the Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn and Neptune. Saturn carries its rings, and Jupiter and Neptune show moving weather as they turn. You will find them together in the Into Space series in Places.

The planet textures are credited on the asset rights page.

Event Horizon.

Two new black-hole Views, Event Horizon: Ring and Event Horizon: Disc, surround you with a lensed starry sky and a glowing accretion disc whose far side bends up over the dark shadow. The sky drifts slowly; with Reduce Motion or Animated View off you see the still panorama.

Want to build your own? Make your own black hole View walks through the free tools step by step.

A sun that answers your gaze.

The sun in space Views, Low Orbit Earth and daylight Views now has a soft glare, and in space it washes out the stars close to it. Look at the sun and the glare grows stronger. visionOS handles this highlight itself, so Locus never learns where you are looking. When a planet passes in front of the sun, its glare disappears.

Places, grouped into series.

Related Places now fold into a single series card, such as Into Space and the Room series At the Café, so a batch of new arrivals no longer fills the first screen. Choose a series to open it inside Places, then choose ‹ Places to return where you were. Searching for a series name finds its members, and searching or filtering shows each member as its own card again.

Library Rooms and Views added in the last 14 days carry a New badge, and a series card shows how many of its members are new.

Turning is on the pad in Move Seat.

In Move Seat and when you place a seat of your own, the main pad now moves you forward and back and turns you left and right. Sideways steps are on the right-hand card, as Move left / right next to Up / down.

Downloads that keep going.

Library downloads now continue while you take off Apple Vision Pro, and Places stays steady when the headset locks and unlocks. A very large download on a slow connection can still pause while Locus is suspended; it picks up again when you return.

Sign in to websites once more.

The Browser now keeps website data in a new, more protected store. After you update, websites you were signed in to in Locus ask you to sign in one more time.
