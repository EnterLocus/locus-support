Locus 1.4.1

Choose music for your Room.

Choose music for your Room and keep it close with the Music Quick Control.

Available now for Apple Vision Pro

About this update

Locus 1.4.1 is now available on the App Store. It requires visionOS 27. Check your installed version in Settings → Help & About → About Locus.

A clearer way to browse

Browse Places with clearer series pages and catalog refresh controls. This update also refines sunlight and shadows, makes Real Objects updates easier to manage, and improves reliability.

Bring your own Room music

Your own Room ZIP can include one ambient music track. Use sound version 2 with role: "music", one looping clip, and ambient placement. The imported Room adds its track to the music you can choose in Virtual Space; Room Portal does not play music.

The Room music reference covers complete Package v2 and flat Room ZIPs, the Locus 1.4.1 minimum version, and the limits checked on import. The public flat ZIP packer and validator accept the same Room music profile.
