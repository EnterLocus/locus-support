Locus 1.4.1

Choose the music for your Room.

Global music brings a choice of tracks to your Room, with its own volume and a Music shortcut in Quick Controls.

Available now for Apple Vision Pro

A soundtrack you choose

In Virtual Space, open Volume → Music to choose from music in your installed Rooms, or select No music. The same controls are in Settings → Current Place → Sound.

Under When a Room has no music, choose Off, Random or a specific track as your default. This preference is saved on your device.

Its own volume, a quick switch

Adjust Music volume separately from Room and View sound. Use Music in Quick Controls to turn music off or back on without opening the full sound controls.

When you change your surroundings

A track you choose for this session stays selected when you change the View or enter a Room without its own music. Entering a different Room with its own music ends your current pick and uses that Room’s music instead.

Music is available in Virtual Space with a Room. It is not available in Room Portal.

Locus 1.4.1 is available on the App Store.
