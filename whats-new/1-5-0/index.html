Locus 1.5.0 for Apple Vision Pro

Watch TV in your Room

Browse and watch on a Room TV. Try the Sky Room TV in Hilltop Villa, and the living room painting above the fireplace—it’s a TV too.

From day into night

Watch the light and sky change throughout the day in supported Views.

For authors

Complete Package v2 Rooms can declare up to four interactive TVs with tvs (requiring Locus 1.4.9 or later), and Views can combine sunPath.panoramaBlend with shore or per-video media.dayNight lighting and emission maps (requiring Locus 1.4.11 or later). Follow the Add a TV guide and Day-and-night View guide, or read the Complete Package v2 guide and format specification.
