Locus 1.6.1 for Apple Vision Pro

Adaptive Performance & smoother rendering

Locus now automatically trims background effects during movement, toolbar summon, or heavy load to keep interaction smooth while preserving sun shadows. You can adjust this anytime in Settings. Optimized sun-path lighting, Room shadow rendering, and foliage shaders further improve frame rates across complex Rooms and Views.

Room audio & TV playback fixes

Room Music now pauses automatically while Room TV audio is playing, and TV playback stops immediately when switching Rooms.

Learn more about Locus on the App Store.
