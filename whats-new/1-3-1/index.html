Locus 1.3.1

Smoother Places, clearer Real Objects.

A smaller update with smoother Places, clearer Real Objects information and more reliable Library updates.

Release archive

Smoother Places

Cards appear progressively to keep browsing responsive. Names and descriptions are more complete in your language.

Clearer Real Objects

Model details show confirmed source, author and license information, warn about known visionOS requirements that your device does not meet, and explain load failures. Straw options appear only when a separate straw variant is available.

More natural water

Reflections, shoreline transitions and underwater detail are refined in Views updated to use these capabilities.

More reliable Library updates

Library updates recover more reliably after an interruption, including updates to the Place you are in.

Easier control editing

In Settings → Workspace, Edit Quick Controls and Customize Bar open as full pages. Enabled controls appear first; dragging a switched-off control among them turns it on.

Locus 1.3.1 supported visionOS 26. Locus 1.4 and later require visionOS 27.
