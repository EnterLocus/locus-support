Locus 1.2.2

A steadier, faster load.

A small update: a crash fix for Views with 3D scenery, and quicker loading for Rooms and Views with baked lighting and water.

Turning a Room off no longer crashes.

In Views with 3D scenery, such as Alpine Lakeshore, turning the Room off could crash Locus. That is fixed. You can switch a Room off and on again in these Views without a problem.

Rooms and Views open faster.

Rooms and Views with baked lighting and water now load faster. You will notice it most in places such as Lakeside Cabin and Alpine Lakeshore, where the lighting, lake and scenery used to be prepared one after another and now load together.
