Locus 1.4.2

Step anywhere in your Room.

Look at the floor and pinch to move there, and switch Views in the same Room with a smoother transition.

Available now for Apple Vision Pro

Look at the floor to move

In Seats, look at the floor and pinch to move there. Back to Seat takes you back, or save the spot as a new seat to keep it.

Smoother View changes

Switching to another View in the same Room now has a smoother transition.

Fixes and reliability

Places details no longer show an out-of-date download or In Use state. This update also improves reliability.

Locus 1.4.2 is available on the App Store.
