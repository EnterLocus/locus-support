Locus 1.1

More ways to make your workspace yours.

Turn a panorama you find on the web into a View, then organize and tune the place around you.

Bring a 360° View straight from the web.

When Locus Browser opens a high-resolution 2:1 panorama, you can preview it in your Room right away. Save the ones you want and they become Views you can return to from Places.

This turns discovering scenery on the web into one continuous flow: find it, try it around you, and keep it in Locus.

Keep every Place in order.

Rename imported Views, mark favorites, add tags, and filter or sort your Rooms and Views. The Library adds more Free and Supporter Views, while On Device keeps downloaded content easy to find.

Shape the room around the View.

Supported Rooms can expose their own lights. Switch them on, then adjust brightness, warmth, and color to suit the View outside. Sunlight temperature also supports Auto and Manual control.

Keep the web close.

Customize the Browser Start Page and save any page with the star in the address bar. Browser windows are larger, remember their size, and make it easier to return to the web tools you use in your workspace.
