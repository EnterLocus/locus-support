Locus 1.1.3

Bring your Views to life.

Animated Views add localized motion and ambient sound to the scenery around your workspace.

Still a photograph. Now alive in the places that matter.

Animated Views keep the photographic world around you while bringing selected regions into motion. Waterfalls can flow, waves can roll in, and foliage can move without turning the whole scene into a video wall.

The motion stays part of the View, so you can pair it with any Room just as you would a still panorama.

Hear the scene around you.

Supported Animated Views can include their own ambient sound, such as falling water, ocean waves, birds, or wind. The sound belongs to the scene and plays with the View rather than with a separate browser or media window.

Know which Views move.

Not every View is animated. Animated Views are clearly marked in Places and the Library, while the rest remain still panoramas. Availability varies as the Library grows.
