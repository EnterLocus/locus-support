Place packages

Bring your own View or Room.

The fastest way to add your own View needs no packaging at all: a single 360° photo. The Locus Place ZIP format below is for sharing a finished View or Room with full metadata.

Package format v1 · Package model v2
The simple way: import one photo. In Locus, open Imports and choose a full 360° panorama (JPG, PNG, or HEIC, 2:1 equirectangular) from Photos or Files. Locus packages it for you — no ZIP, no manifests, no tools. Everything below is only for the shareable package format.
Start small. Publish one reusable View, Room, or Experience per ZIP. An Experience is the composition that pairs a View with a Room and declares its supported mode.

Archive layout

my-place.zip
├── locusplace.json
└── catalog/
    ├── destinations/<view-id>/
    │   ├── destination.json
    │   ├── provenance.json
    │   └── panorama.jpg
    ├── spaces/<room-id>/
    │   ├── space.json
    │   ├── provenance.json
    │   └── scene.usdz
    └── experiences/<experience-id>/
        └── experience.json

The `.zip` and `.locusplace` filename extensions use the same bytes and rules. The archive must not contain an extra parent folder, symlinks, undeclared files, authoring sources such as `.blend`, or paths outside the declared packages.

Provenance is part of the package

Every imported View and Room needs a provenance.json document. It records who made the asset and what can truthfully be said about it.

FieldExamples
Creator and sourcecreatorOrAgency, sourcePageURL

Do not invent an author or license. Unknown or incomplete mandatory provenance fails validation. Public-domain or CC0 material should still preserve its known creator or agency and source.

Create and validate

  1. Download or generate the reproducible starter archives.
  2. Unzip one archive into a normal directory and replace the panorama or USDZ.
  3. Edit the Package-v2 manifests and complete provenance.json truthfully.
  4. Run the packer to regenerate file sizes and hashes.
  5. Run the validator before sharing or importing the finished ZIP.
python3 examples/build_examples.py /tmp/locusplace-examples
python3 tools/pack_locusplace.py MyEditablePlace MyPlace.zip

Asset guidance

View

Use a decoded JPEG or PNG panorama with a strict 2:1 equirectangular projection. 12,288 × 6,144 is recommended, not required. Forward is panorama u = 0.5, world −Z; the seam is yaw ±180°. An optional image-based-lighting EXR may accompany the panorama to light Room geometry; HDR visible-sky assets are no longer part of the format and are rejected by the validator.

Room

Use a self-contained, meter-scale USDZ with +Y up. Declare a seated origin, a safe head volume, destination openings, collision behavior, and measured quality metadata. Keep `.blend`, GLB, source textures, and other authoring assets outside the runtime ZIP.

Experience

Reference stable View and Room IDs, declare supported modes, and provide the composition metadata required by that mode. Locus does not infer a pairing from filenames.
