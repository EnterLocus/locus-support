Build a View.

Turn an idea into the 360° scenery around your Room. Follow one Maldives panorama through two AI enhancement routes, with real images, native sizes, repairs and costs.

Start your own View

Clone the public project, open the folder in an agent that can generate images and run Python, and ask it to use the build-a-view skill. The host’s built-in image tool can make the initial base without an external API key; host usage limits still apply. High-resolution enhancement needs your selected provider’s API key.

git clone https://github.com/EnterLocus/locus-support.git
cd locus-support
Prompt for your agentUse the build-a-view skill to make a static full-sphere View of a place I describe. First show me the base panorama and a 360° preview where supported. Wait for me to accept the layout. Then offer models, native-size estimates, Standard or Batch processing, and an itemized cost plan before any paid enhancement.

There are two checkpoints: accept the base and its layout first; then choose the API model, credential reference, native-size estimate, cost plan and processing mode. Give an environment-variable name such as OPENAI_API_KEY or GEMINI_API_KEY, never a secret in chat. Approve each concrete paid submission, or explicitly authorize a listed full plan under a combined cap. A key or a planning budget alone is not approval.

A 360° preview depends on your host. If it cannot open one, ask for an available local interactive preview and perspective views. A flat image alone does not show what every direction looks like.

Use the defaults below: restore the whole low-resolution base, enhance six contextual faces, merge at actual native sampling density, then inspect every direction, join and pole. Start with no repairs and reserve up to two targeted outputs; extra attempts need their own scope and approval.

One original, two routes

This comparison starts from the same unchanged 1774×887 built-in image. The prompt requested a lagoon-facing center and a clean wrap; the base instead centers palms and has a wrap defect. Those choices were accepted and frozen for this comparison. Enhancement reconstructs plausible detail; it does not recover photographic truth.

Read the exact original prompt
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a full spherical immersive skybox source, not a wide-angle photograph. Create one full 360-degree by 180-degree equirectangular panorama in an exact 2:1 canvas, 3840x1920 if supported. A serene Maldives island beach at eye level about 1.5m above fine white sand, looking out over luminous shallow turquoise water toward a clear level Indian Ocean horizon. In the opposite direction, the same beach continues into natural tropical palms and low lush island vegetation. No buildings, people, boats, text or logos. Warm clear midmorning daylight, realistic white sand, delicate small lapping waves, convincing individual palm fronds and natural vegetation. Full 360 longitude, full latitude from zenith to nadir. Camera has zero roll, horizon is perfectly level at exactly vertical center (50% height); beach and water are below center. Equirectangular longitude distortion and strong polar stretching are required; the entire top row is the single blue sky zenith, the entire bottom row the single sandy nadir directly under camera, with natural pole convergence when projected onto a sphere. The left and right borders must join as one continuous scene, with identical sky, horizon, ocean/beach geometry and lighting at the wrap; place the wrap in uncomplicated distant water/sky if possible. One coherent island with geometrically continuous shoreline and a calm comfortable seated-view atmosphere. Sharp detailed natural photographic rendering, restrained color and exposure; no oversharpening, no lens vignette, no collage, no inset views, no frame.

GPT Image 2.6 uses API model gpt-image-2.6-sunburst; Nano Banana Pro uses gemini-3-pro-image. Both received the same source bytes. The comparison images below put GPT on the left and Nano Banana on the right.

Restore the whole panorama first

A coherent global restoration comes before fine face detail. GPT returned 3840×1920 at high quality in Standard mode. Nano Banana used cyclic 21:9 context in Standard 4K, returned 6336×2688, and was explicitly unpadded and downsampled to a 5376×2688 panorama. The source stays unchanged.

Six faces, measured native size

Project six approximately 102° faces: a 90° core with 6° context on each side. GPT’s 1186-square inputs returned 2880-square faces; Nano Banana’s 1660-square inputs returned 4096-square faces. The inputs were projected at source density, not enlarged to the requested output size. The successful probe is one of the six faces.

Merge the returned faces directly with a 0.25° blend half-width. Calculate the final 2:1 size from the actual stored-to-core ratio T and returned face side N. This conservative sampling rule describes pixel density, not resolved detail. Neither result is native 12K.

f_center = N / (2*T) pixels per radian
W = 2 * floor(pi*N / (2*T)); H = W/2

GPT:  T=1186/960,  N=2880 → 7322×3661
Nano: T=1660/1344, N=4096 → 10418×5209

These are 512-pixel crops from the native forward faces. Open the image at full size for 1:1 inspection. They cover different angles because the pixel densities differ; the matched-angle comparison below is better for judging the same scenery.

Repair what the images actually show

GPT’s raw merge had sky facets and vertical exposure bands. Local sky-tone matching reduced the facets, and a guarded mask retained coherent global-restoration sky over about one third of the canvas. That area interpolates the 3840×1920 sky rather than adding new 7.3K detail. Two paid 60° perspective edits at yaw ±135° repaired the remaining sky/water join bands.

Nano Banana’s nadir had a radial sand star. The first paid repair failed and still counts in the cost. Local donor-grain healing removed the radial shape; a second paid edit restored grain, localized to protect stones, shadows and overlaps. A local wrap correction reduced the narrow sky seam.

Nano Banana still has a broad sky-tone split at the wrap, converging cirrus at the zenith, and smoother central nadir relief. In these outputs, GPT reconstructed crisper foliage, water and sand; Nano Banana returned more pixels with softer detail. More pixels do not guarantee a better View.

The matched-angle images use the same directions, field of view and 768-pixel review grid. They are perspective resamplings, not extra generated detail. Review covered six directions, all 12 cube-edge views, poles, wrap and repair boundaries. These remain comparison candidates: Locus import, physical Vision Pro appearance and comfort are not verified. The water is painted into a static skybox, with no motion, depth or parallax.

Count the whole job before paying

Count paid outputs as B + G + F + R + E: paid bases, global restorations, faces, repairs and extra attempts. Here B=0, G=1, F=6, R=2, E=0: nine per route and 18 total. The accepted probe belongs to F6. A rejected repair is still a paid output; further rerolls increase the count.

Download, inspect, then import

The delivery JPEGs keep the native dimensions, using quality 95 and 4:4:4 sampling. Download either candidate, save it in iCloud Drive/Locus, and wait until Files on Apple Vision Pro can open it. In Locus, open Places → Import → Import a View → Files, then select the JPEG. Check the wrap, poles, scale and comfort in the headset before accepting your own result.
