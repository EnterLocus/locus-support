Room creation example

Alpine Scriptorium, from concept to headset review.

The original accepted Sol 1.1.1 Room experiment: its brief, concept, editable source, exact import archive, creator-supplied Apple Vision Pro screenshots, credits, and unresolved defects.

Start with the record

This is one preserved creator workflow, not a benchmark or a recommendation of a particular model. It publishes the exact original Sol 1.1.1 result rather than the later baked-light experiment.

Known issues are part of the example. The lamps flicker, some wall sconces float away from their walls, and their glow does not bake onto the room’s surrounding surfaces. The mountain View is a manipulated partial panorama, not a finished 360° capture.

The original brief and concept

The creator’s original Chinese brief was: 山中的一个大书库，很高的哥特式建筑。玻璃窗外是美丽的山景和远处的雪山。

Translation: A large library in the mountains, a very tall Gothic building, with a beautiful mountain landscape and distant snow-capped mountains outside the glass windows.

The English agent brief, View feedback, and later refinement feedback were working instructions, not the original creator prompt. The later refinement asked for a closer practical match after the initial handoff. The exact image-generation prompt for this concept was not retained, so it is not reconstructed or presented here as a quotation.

The Sol 1.1.1 result

The package contains four seats, a separately selectable 8192 × 4096 View, and an Experience. Its public Mac package check passed with warnings, which is useful structural evidence but not an Apple Vision Pro rendering, comfort, or performance result.

What the creator saw on Apple Vision Pro

The creator imported and viewed the exact hashed Sol 1.1.1 archive on Apple Vision Pro. These supplied screenshots show that review; they do not establish frame rate, tracking, comfort, interaction behavior, or the cause of a defect.

Known issues, kept visible

  • No baked lamp light on receiving surfaces. Fixtures glow, but this version has no baked lamp contribution on the floor, table, walls, or ceiling.
  • Lamp flicker. The creator observed flicker on Apple Vision Pro. Its cause, frequency, and a repair are not established, and a still image cannot establish it.
  • Detached sconces. Several wall lamps are visibly separated from their walls.
  • Interpreted View. The mountain View comes from a partial panorama whose rear is mirrored, sky and ground are extended, and nadir resolves to tan ground. It is not a polished, naturally captured 360° View.

A focused source and exported-USDZ geometry preflight detects opposing near-coplanar shade faces and shade/piping overlap in the original lamp. The repaired USDZ has no finding in its shade, frame, and brass-object scan. This is a useful hypothesis for one lamp, not a whole-Room check and not proof that Apple Vision Pro flicker is fixed.

This is a preserved accepted result, not a claim that the issues are repaired. A repair belongs in the editable Blender source, followed by a new package/content version and another device review under recorded conditions.

Historical combined import, current separate workflow

AlpineScriptorium-Sol-1.1.1.locusplace is an older combined Package v2 archive: importing it brings in its Room, selectable View, and Experience together, and removing it removes that one imported package. Its bytes are preserved because they are what the creator reviewed.

For new work, package a Room and a View separately so they can be updated and removed independently. Follow the four-step Room tutorial and the complete Package v2 guide; this historical combined archive is not the current template.
