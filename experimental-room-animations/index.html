Free animated Room

Try a Room that moves.

Cloud Fan Pavilion is a sheltered timber Room with a broad-leaf plant that moves continuously in the breeze.

Free · Locus Place 1.0.1 · 22.2 MB · Requires Locus 1.1.1 or later
All animation features shown here are experimental. This includes the Room animation metadata, playback behavior, Controls and Quick Settings surfaces, speed control, and replay intervals. They may change and should not yet be treated as a stable production authoring contract.

Download and try it

Cloud Fan Pavilion is free in the Locus Library. You can also download the same Locus Place here and open it with Locus.

Download Cloud Fan Pavilion

  1. Open the downloaded .locusplace file with Locus.
  2. Enter Cloud Fan Pavilion and open Controls → Ambient Animations to turn Plant Breeze on or off.
  3. Open Quick Settings → Room to try experimental speed and interval settings, then save them for this Room.

Turning Plant Breeze off stops the movement; the Room remains available. Saved animation settings apply only to this Room on the current device.

What moves

Plant BreezeThe pavilion's broad leaves sway in a loop. Its 0–0 second interval repeats immediately, so the movement stays continuous.
Your paceAdjust the animation from half speed to one-and-a-half speed, or switch it off while keeping the Room available.

How experimental intervals work

The interval is the pause after one animation finishes. A minimum and maximum of 0–0 means immediate replay. Matching nonzero values create a fixed pause. With different values, Locus picks a fresh random pause within the range after every completed play.

The minimum never crosses above the maximum: raising the minimum also raises the maximum when needed, and lowering the maximum also lowers the minimum. Speed and interval changes are experimental along with the rest of Room animation playback.

For Room creators

This download is a working Locus Place example of the experimental ambientAnimations field. The building, animated plant, and animation clip are packaged together in one self-contained USDZ.
