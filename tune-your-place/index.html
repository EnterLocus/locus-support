Basics · 10 minutes

Fine-tune your Place.

Turn a View toward the scene you want, balance the Room lighting, then make favorite Places easy to find again.

On this pageAdjust a ViewUse Room LightsOrganize Places

Adjust a View

  1. Open Places.
  2. Open the menu for the View, then choose Edit View. Locus opens a live preview so you can see changes in the Room.
  3. Turn the View. Put the subject or direction you want in front of the selected seat.
  4. Balance the image. Adjust View Brightness, Contrast, and Color.
  5. Balance the Room. Adjust Room Lighting separately. Add Sunlight only when the panorama has a clear directional light that the Room should share.

These adjustments are saved for that View and return whenever you use it with another Room.

Use Room Lights

Room Lights appear only in Rooms that declare visitor-controlled fixtures.

  1. Enter a supported Virtual Space.
  2. Open Controls and switch on Room Lights. The Room's own lamps and authored indirect layer respond together.
  3. Open Quick Settings, then choose Room. Adjust the brightness, warmth, or color offered by each light group. The available ranges come from the Room.
  4. Choose Save for This Room… to keep the current light settings for this Room on this device.
Session or saved? Until you choose Save for This Room…, your changes last only for the current session. A dusk or night View can suggest that Room Lights start on, but you remain in control.

Organize Places

  • Rename an imported View when its filename is not useful. Built-in Views keep their published names.
  • Mark a Room or View as a Favorite to bring it forward with Favorites First.
  • Add tags such as Work, Night, Nature, or Shared.
  • Search and filter by name, Favorites, Imported, resolution, or tag.
  • Sort by Title, Recently Added, or Favorites First.

Names, favorites, and tags organize the library. They do not change the imported panorama or Room files.
