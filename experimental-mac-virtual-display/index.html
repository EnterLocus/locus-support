Use Mac Virtual Display in a Locus Place.

Connect your Mac before you enter Virtual Space, then keep its screen beside your Locus Browser windows.

Requires Apple Vision Pro Developer Mode

Check the normal Apple requirements first

Make sure Mac Virtual Display works outside Locus before changing a developer setting. Your Mac and Apple Vision Pro need compatible software, the same Apple Account with two-factor authentication, and the connectivity settings in Apple's Mac Virtual Display guide.

This setup is optional. Locus's own Browser windows, Views, Rooms, and Places work normally without Developer Mode or Mac Virtual Display.

1 · Enable

Allow Mac Virtual Display in immersive apps

  1. Pair Apple Vision Pro with Xcode on your Mac. Follow Apple's Developer Mode guide.
  2. Turn on Developer Mode on Apple Vision Pro. Restart and confirm when visionOS asks.
  3. Open Settings → Developer.
  4. Turn on Allow Mac Virtual Display.

Developer Mode is a device-wide Apple setting intended for development. Turn it on only if you are comfortable with that tradeoff.

2 · Connect

Open Mac Virtual Display first

  1. Open Control Center on Apple Vision Pro.
  2. Connect to your Mac with Mac Virtual Display. Wait until the Mac window is visible and usable.
  3. Open Locus and enter your Place.
  4. Move the Mac window where it is comfortable. visionOS owns the window, so use its normal controls.
The order matters. If you open Mac Virtual Display after entering a Locus virtual environment, visionOS exits that environment. Reconnect to Mac Virtual Display first, then enter the Locus Place again.

Know what Locus controls

visionOS—not Locus—creates, places, streams, and controls the Mac window. Locus cannot restore the connection, choose its resolution, or move the window for you. If the Mac window disappears or disconnects, exit the Place, reconnect from Control Center, then enter the Place again.
