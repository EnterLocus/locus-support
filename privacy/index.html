Locus privacy notice

This notice explains how Locus handles room information, browsing data, imported Places, purchases, and support messages.

Last updated September 5, 2026

Spatial and room information

Locus uses room information from visionOS to line up a 3D Room with your desk and open Room Portal in the right place. This work happens on your Apple Vision Pro.

visionOS controls camera and sensor access. Locus uses room information on the device to position and reveal content.

Browser activity

Websites you open in Locus can collect information under their own privacy policies, just as they can in another browser. Locus uses Apple's WebKit browser, which may store cookies and other website data on your device. You can clear your history and website data in Locus.

Imported Places and local library data

Place packages that you choose from Files or a supported URL are validated before installation and stored in the app's local library. Deleting an imported Place removes Locus's installed copy; it does not delete the source file from Files or your cloud-storage provider.

Package metadata can include creator, source, license, credits, modification notes, and AI-generation notes. That metadata is shown so people can understand where a View or Room came from.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store purchases and subscriptions. Locus receives the purchase status needed to unlock what you bought; it does not receive your full payment-card details.

Library downloads and Supporter verification

Locus downloads the Library catalog and View packages from services hosted with Cloudflare. To authorize a Supporter download, the app sends the requested package identifier and Apple-signed purchase information to the Locus Library service. The service verifies the subscription with Apple and returns a temporary download link. Apple processes the purchase; Locus does not receive your payment-card details.

The authorization service processes the signed purchase information and transaction identifiers in memory for the request. It does not write them or the temporary download links to application logs. For abuse prevention, it derives a one-way identifier from the purchase transaction family and uses Cloudflare's rate-limiting service to count requests in a 60-second window. This identifier is used for download access and security, not advertising or tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Network requests also expose ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses, to the hosting provider as needed to deliver and protect the service. Library downloads do not send your room scans, camera images, browsing history, or imported Place contents to the Locus Library service.

Support and public feedback

Emailing support sends the information you choose to include to the publisher's support mailbox. Public GitHub issues are visible to everyone. Never post private room images, credentials, receipts, order information, account details, notifications, device identifiers, or other personal data in a public issue.

Send private or security-sensitive reports to support@enterlocus.com and include only what is necessary.

Data retention and deletion

Local browser and imported-package data remain on your device until you clear or remove them, subject to visionOS and device backup behavior. Support messages and issue posts are retained as needed to answer, diagnose, prevent abuse, and keep a record of the resolution. You can request deletion of a support message by emailing support; public GitHub content is also subject to GitHub's platform controls and retention.
