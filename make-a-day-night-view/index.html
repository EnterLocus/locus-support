Advanced · Package v2

Make a day-and-night View.

Turn a sun-free 360° panorama into a full 24-hour Place: moving sunlight and sky atmosphere, stars and the Moon at night, graded terrain, and an optional full-frame night panorama that blends in after sunset.

Needs Locus 1.4.9 – 1.4.11 or later · Complete Package v2
On this pageChoose your approach1 · Prepare the images2 · Write destination.json3 · Tune stops and blend4 · Pack, validate, and testCoexistence and limits

Choose your approach

Day-and-night Views build on environment.sunPath inside a complete Package v2 archive (the flat compatibility view.json ZIP does not support these fields). The manifest below follows Chalk Dune Dawn (destination.chalk-dune-dawn 1.4.0), a 12K gypsum-dunes View pairing full-frame day and night panoramas, procedural clear sky, stars/Milky Way, and sun-lit 3D midground dunes.

Study the Chalk Dune Dawn example. Download destination.chalk-dune-dawn-1.4.0.locusplace (123.9 MB ZIP under the Locus Example License; also listed in place-examples.json). Needs Locus 1.4.10 / 1.5.0 or later, and see Chalk Dune Dawn in the Gallery.

Why sky and daySky are part of the contract. In the current Package v2 specification, panoramaBlend requires both environment.sunPath.daySky and root sky (with sky.mask and stars or moon). White pixels in sky.mask show the rendered day atmosphere and night stars/Milky Way; black pixels show your blended day and night panorama images.

1 · Prepare

Prepare the images and masks

  • Daytime panorama (panorama.path): A 2:1 equirectangular JPEG or PNG (for example 8192×4096 or 12288×6144), lit for clear daylight and completely free of a painted sun or sun glare.
  • Sun-free Room lighting image (environment.imageBasedLight): A separate 2:1 JPEG or PNG (1024×512 recommended), also free of a painted sun.
  • Sky mask (sky.mask): A 2:1 8-bit grayscale PNG (PNG colour type 0) aligned to the panorama. Paint the open sky white (255) and land, trees, and buildings black (0), with clean anti-aliased edges along horizons and branches.
  • Optional sun-disc mask (environment.sunPath.disc.skyMask): A 2:1 8-bit grayscale PNG (max width 4096) where white admits the moving solar disc behind foreground edges.
  • Aligned night panorama (panoramaBlend.path, for 1.4.10+): A separate 2:1 JPEG or PNG with the exact same pixel width and height as panorama.path, keeping the identical horizon, mountains, and structures under night relighting.
  • ASTC GPU sidecars (gpuTextures v2, required for panoramaBlend): Convert both panorama.path and panoramaBlend.path to astc-6x6-srgb KTX1 files (.ktx) with full mipmaps and repeating horizontal wrap. Record each file’s sourceSHA256, dimensions, and mipmapLevels. Legacy singular gpuTexture is rejected when panoramaBlend is present.
Preserving painted clouds while keeping procedural sky and stars. Put clear open sky (255) in sky.mask and keep painted clouds black (0) with a gentle edge ramp so clouds stay opaque over the water and sky without dark cutout halos; then supply continuous cloud-thickness transmittance (high grays in thin wisps, low grays in thick cores, 0 on solid horizons) in environment.sunPath.disc.skyMask so sunlight fades naturally behind clouds. Case study: Tropical Shallow Bay: day and night with painted clouds
Using your own painted sky instead of Locus’s procedural sky. Paint sky.mask completely black (0) while keeping root sky and daySky declared as required. Because black in sky.mask keeps your panoramas opaque, Locus hides the rendered blue sky, stars, and Moon while preserving the 24-hour clock, Edit View Sun Position scrubbing, panoramaBlend transitions, panoramaGrade, and dynamic Room/midground lighting. Omit environment.sunPath.disc (or pass the all-black mask to disc.skyMask) to hide the solar disc and glare as well, or supply a separate white-sky disc.skyMask if you still want the moving sun disc.

2 · Manifest

Write destination.json and provenance

Set minimumAppVersion in locusplace.json to "1.4.9" (without panoramaBlend), "1.4.10" (with panoramaBlend), or "1.4.11" (when pairing panoramaBlend with shore or media.dayNight).

3 · Calibrate

Tune elevation stops and blend thresholds

  • daySky: haze (0...1, default 0.3) raises aerosol glow near the horizon and sun; exposure (0.25...4, default 1) scales the day-sky adaptation curve.
  • panoramaGrade.stops: 2 to 16 stops with strictly increasing elevationDegrees (-90...90) and linear RGB color multipliers (0...4). Use it to dim and warm painted terrain at sunrise/sunset before the night panorama takes over.
  • light.stops: 2 to 16 stops driving the Room’s directional sun (color 0...16 multiplying peakIlluminanceLux) and Room/glass IBL exposure (ambientEV -6...6, default 0). Add "midground": true when the View also carries a 3D midground.path model that should receive the same directional sunlight.
  • panoramaBlend: Cross-fades smoothly from panorama.path at or above dayElevationDegrees (default -1) to panoramaBlend.path at or below nightElevationDegrees (default -12; both in -18...10 with night < day), tinted by linear RGB nightGrade (0...4).

4 · Verify

Pack, validate, and inspect across hours

python3 tools/pack_locusplace.py --current-app-version 1.4.11 \
  /absolute/path/to/MyDayNightView /absolute/path/to/MyDayNightView.locusplace

python3 tools/validate_locusplace.py --current-app-version 1.4.11 \
  /absolute/path/to/MyDayNightView.locusplace

The validator checks every manifest constraint, verifies both gpuTextures v2 KTX1 headers and SHA-256 digests, and runs sun_free_sky_check to catch bright solar discs left in the day panorama or IBL.

  1. Import on Apple Vision Pro via Places → Import → Import a View → Files and pair the View with a Room.
  2. Scrub Sun Position in Edit View through morning, noon, golden hour, twilight, and midnight to check horizon silhouettes, Room glass reflections, and painted-to-sky edge masks.
  3. Check Motion Off / Reduce Motion: confirm the View freezes cleanly at startHour.

Coexistence rules and limits

  • Excluded effects: daySky, panoramaGrade, light, and panoramaBlend cannot coexist with clouds, sky.aurora, midground.water, midground.flowingWater, or legacy video media.
  • Allowed pairings in 1.4.11+ & 1.5.1+: Computed shore surf (locus.view.water.surf.panorama-blend.v1) and media.dayNight video maps (locus.view.media.day-night.v1, providing per-video 8-bit RGB lighting and emission PNGs) pair with panoramaBlend in Locus 1.4.11+; combining 24-hour daySky, panoramaGrade, and light directly with root water and water.contactCalibration (excluding sky.aurora) requires Locus 1.5.1 or later.
  • Both panoramas must match dimensions exactly. Even a one-pixel difference between panorama.path and panoramaBlend.path is rejected.
