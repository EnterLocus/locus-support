Lifetime and Supporter are different

Lifetime permanently unlocks the library you provide. Supporter supports continued development, unlocks unlimited custom imports while active, and is the route to future Locus-authored update packs when they exist.

V1 does not promise a publishing cadence. The current subscription value is support plus the active unlimited-import entitlement; future packs or features are possible, not guaranteed.

What happens after a subscription ends?

Locus does not delete or disable custom imports or subscriber packages you already installed and kept. You cannot add new custom imports above the free allowance or download new subscriber packages until you resubscribe or buy the lifetime import unlock. If you intentionally remove a subscriber package after expiry, downloading it again requires an active subscription.

Storefront and availability

Apple supplies localized prices, taxes, billing terms, cancellation controls, and purchase confirmation. Final products, restore behavior, and App Store availability remain subject to publisher configuration and App Review. This page will be updated if launch pricing changes.
