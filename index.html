Apple Vision Pro

A spatial workspace for Apple Vision Pro.

Use a 360° View with a 3D Room, keep a real desk in the workspace, and open web pages in spatial windows.

Support Create a Place

Pre-release: Locus is in active V1 development and is not currently available for purchase.

Places

Choose a View and a Room.

The launch screen pairs a 360° View with an authored Room, then offers the experience modes supported by that pair.

Virtual Space

Use an authored Room around your desk.

Choose a Room and View, then align the authored desk with the physical desk.

Room Portal

Open one wall of the real room to a View.

This mode keeps the physical room visible and replaces one selected wall.

Spatial browser

Open web pages in spatial windows.

Each window uses WebKit and can contain multiple tabs.

Custom content

Import a View or build a Room package.

Views import directly from a complete 2:1 JPEG, PNG, or HEIC image. Rooms use a documented .locusplace or ZIP package with a USDZ, integrity hashes, and a local validator.

Device requirements

Simulator captures do not replace Vision Pro testing.

Locus uses visionOS spatial tracking to position content. Physical comfort, awareness, tracking, and Room Portal behavior depend on Apple Vision Pro and the surrounding space.
