Create your own Place

Bring your own scenery and 3D spaces.

Import a complete 360° image as a View, or use an exact 2:1 panorama from Locus Browser when the displayed image is at least 4,096 × 2,048. Try it as a session-only preview before saving it. If you build 3D spaces, the authoring guide shows you how to turn one into a Room for Locus.
